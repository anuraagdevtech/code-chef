topic: Binary Search
subtopic: Binary search a monotone sum of ceiling divisions
time: $O(N\log V)$ per test case
space: $O(N)$
complexity_note: Auxiliary space excludes the buffered input and output.
---
At speed $K$, a pile of size $a$ takes $\lceil a/K\rceil$ hours because an unfinished hour cannot be shared with another pile. The total required hours decreases as $K$ increases.

Binary search the smallest feasible speed between one and the largest pile. Use integer ceiling division `(a+K-1)//K`. Since $H\ge N$, the upper endpoint is always feasible.

## Edge cases

- Even a tiny pile consumes a full hour.
- When $H=N$, the answer is the largest pile.
- Use integer arithmetic for large pile sizes.

## Dry run

For piles `[1,2,3]` and `H=4`, speed 1 takes six hours, speed 2 takes `1+1+2=4`, and the minimum is 2.
