topic: Trees
subtopic: Carry the maximum ancestor wealth during iterative DFS
time: $O(N)$ overall
space: $O(N)$
complexity_note: Auxiliary space excludes the buffered input and output.
---
For each employee, the best ancestor to pair with it is the richest ancestor on its root path. Carry that maximum during DFS, evaluate the ancestor-minus-descendant difference before incorporating the current vertex, then propagate the updated maximum to children.

Every valid strict ancestor pair is represented by this maximum at the descendant. Negative wealth needs no special handling. The traversal is iterative to support long management chains.

## Edge cases

- The root is identified by parent `-1`, not by its index.
- Wealth may be negative.
- Initialise the answer below all possible differences, since strict ancestor differences can be negative.

## Dry run

The sample has root 2 with wealth 10, and descendant 3 under vertex 4 of wealth 12. At vertex 3, highest ancestor wealth is 12 and its wealth is 6, giving the maximum difference 6.
