# Problem-2 (SET302)

[Problem statement](https://www.codechef.com/problems/SET302) · [Python solution](../../solutions/SET302.py)

## Approach

For a fixed hourly rate K, the required hours are the sum of ceil(A_i/K). This predicate is monotone, so binary-search the first feasible rate between the average lower bound and the largest pile.

## Complexity

- Time: O(N log max A).
- Space: O(N).
