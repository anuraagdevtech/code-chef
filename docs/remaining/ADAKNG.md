# Ada King (ADAKNG)

[Problem statement](https://www.codechef.com/problems/ADAKNG) · [Python solution](../../solutions/ADAKNG.py)

## Approach

A king reaches exactly the cells whose row and column differ by at most K. Clip this square to the 8-by-8 board and multiply its row and column extents.

## Complexity

- Time: O(1).
- Space: O(1).
