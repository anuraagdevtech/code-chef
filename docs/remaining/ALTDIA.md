# ALTDIA

One vertex has diameter zero and two vertices need opposite colors. For at least three vertices, a star has the minimum possible diameter two. Choose its center to have the color opposite a color with at least two vertices, giving an alternating leaf-center-leaf diameter. A monochromatic nontrivial tree is impossible.

Complexity: O(B+W) time and output space.

Validation: repository sample checks and deterministic differential checks where applicable; no online acceptance is claimed.
