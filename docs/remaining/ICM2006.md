# Lavanya Loves DFA (ICM2006)

[Problem statement](https://www.codechef.com/problems/ICM2006) · [Python solution](../../solutions/ICM2006.py)

## Approach

Simulate the five-state automaton shown in the statement diagram. Reading one sends every city to the blue city; zero stays at red or advances blue-yellow-pink-green-red. Accept only when the final city is green.

## Complexity

- Time: O(length).
- Space: O(length).
