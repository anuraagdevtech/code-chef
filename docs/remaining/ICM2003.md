# ICM2003

Binary-search the zero of the derivative numerator on (0,pi/2).

Correctness: The numerator H=(2x+b)sin(x)-(x squared+bx+c)cos(x) has derivative (2+x squared+bx+c)sin(x)>0. Its endpoint signs differ, so the unique zero is the global minimum.

Complexity: O(55) time and O(1) space per case.

Validation: official samples, semantic construction checks where needed, and deterministic differential regressions in `tools/test_advanced_one.py`. No online judge acceptance is claimed.
