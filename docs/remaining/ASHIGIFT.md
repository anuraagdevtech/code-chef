# ASHIGIFT

Reverse the journey while maintaining the minimum group needed to finish the remaining suffix. A dish adds its cost. A clan may be skipped, or joined if its threshold is met, giving the minimum of need and max(threshold,need-recruits). These are exact inverse feasibility thresholds.

Complexity: O((B+C) log(B+C)) time and O(B+C) space.

Validation: repository sample checks and deterministic differential checks where applicable; no online acceptance is claimed.
